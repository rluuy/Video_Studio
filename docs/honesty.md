# What a picture may claim

An animation is believed more easily than a paper, so it has to be stricter. These rules come first when they collide
with a nicer picture.

## Four kinds of content, always told apart on the frame

| Kind | Means | The frame says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measured | data someone recorded | "measured", whose, and what was done to it |
| Computed | the output of a calculation in `export.py` or in the page | what was computed, from which rule |
| Predicted | what a model says will happen | "predicted", "as planned", "for a perfect run" |
| Schematic | drawn to explain | "schematic", "not to scale", "drawn larger" |

- A prediction is never shown as a result, and the two never share a counter.
- A scene that shows an ideal run says so, above all after a scene about why real runs fall short.
- Schematic and measured things on one frame are each labeled.

## Geometry is computed, or it is called a sketch

- Rays obey reflection on the surface that is drawn. Gaps are distances. Sums are sums. Fringes come from the height map.
- What is drawn larger than life has its factor on the same frame, for as long as it is larger. The factor changes only
  as visible motion.
- Light drawn on a magnified surface is labeled as drawn on the magnified surface, and so are the numbers read from it.
- Color limits are symmetric for signed quantities, and printed. A map that is clipped says so.
- A claim the picture makes is also asserted in code ("the orbit footprint is flat to 10 mm"), so it stays true when
  something changes.

## Numbers

- Every number on screen or in the narration is a row of `facts.json`, or is marked as drawn with the reason.
- A number keeps its conditions. "After active optics", "over a 14 cm mirror", "two matrices together".
- The same quantity has the same name, unit and rounding everywhere.
- A counter that runs while nothing measures it is schematic and says so.
- Two numbers side by side must be the same kind of number. A maker's figure is labeled as the maker's.

## Words that go wrong

- "Ran out of memory" only if it was tried. Otherwise "not built".
- "Falls back", "automatically", "always", "steady", "exact", "best possible" only if literally so.
- "Measured, not guessed" makes computing sound like guessing.
- Whose method, whose computer, whose data, each said plainly. Work by others is credited where it is used.
- "First" and "invented" need a source that says it.
- Organizations and programs are named only as facts, as small source labels. Tools are drawn generic, without logos.

## People and data

- Unpublished and co-author data only when Ryan says so for this video. The brief lists it and the answer is recorded.
- No voice clones of real people, and no likeness of one.
- Simulated data is never styled to pass as a measurement.
- Other people's figures are not copied. They are redrawn from their numbers, with the source.

## Symbols and units

SI units, the symbols the field uses, defined where they first appear. One symbol has one meaning in a video.
